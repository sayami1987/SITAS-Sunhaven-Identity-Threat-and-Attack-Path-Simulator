# Identity and Access Management Policy

## Purpose

To ensure that access to Sunhaven systems and protected information is based on an authorised identity and a legitimate work requirement.

## Policy

Sunhaven should:

- provide individual identities for users;
- verify identity before granting access;
- grant only the access needed for an approved role;
- review access when responsibilities change;
- remove access when it is no longer required;
- protect sensitive and privileged functions with stronger controls;
- avoid unnecessary shared identities.

Access should follow the principle of least privilege.

## SITAS Alignment

SITAS models identities, credentials, sessions, devices, applications, privileges and protected information as nodes in a directed graph.

The simulator does not provision or manage real accounts. Its purpose is to analyse possible identity-related attack paths and demonstrate the security effect of relevant controls.

**Verified status:** Aligned in the simulator; operational IAM enforcement is outside SITAS scope.
